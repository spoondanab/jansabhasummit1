Sandeep Jhawar

Corporate & Soft Skills Trainer

With 20+ years of experience across corporate, MSME, skill development, soft skills, entrepreneurship development and POSH training, Sandeep Jhawar brings extensive experience in mentoring and training young minds.

He has delivered training and coaching on 70+ topics across 40 different sectors and has successfully trained 20,000+ students across India. He has also conducted 60+ guest lectures and seminars as a motivational speaker and has actively worked with colleges, NGOs and various organisations.

At the Jan Sabha Sansad Leadership Bootcamp, he will share his experience and practical insights to help participants build confidence, communication, leadership and career skills.
