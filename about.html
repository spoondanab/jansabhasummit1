The Agenda & Significance

Examine the foundational principles driving Nagpur's most historic policy deliberation.

Sole Official Agenda:

“To deliberate upon the proposal to abolish the reservation system and administrative categories in India and to examine the feasibility of establishing a uniform system founded on the principles of equal opportunity and merit.”

Why This Topic, Why Now?

India stands at a crucial socio-economic juncture where legislative policies must be continuously critically re-evaluated. Jan Sabha Sansad provides a neutral, high-level institutional forum to dissect administrative categories and evaluate whether modern governance requires a transition toward a purely merit-driven, equal-opportunity framework.

Commitment to Meritocracy

Our philosophy rests on empowering individuals through intellect and evidence-based debate. By bridging policy research with parliamentary practice, JSS moves delegates “From learning → to leading the house”, ensuring every participant rises purely based on strategic acumen and logical rigour.
